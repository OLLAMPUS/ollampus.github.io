Dream Room Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Dream Room (the "app"), made by Ollampus ("we", "us"), uses and why. We keep it short: we do not run our own servers, we do not ask you to create an account with us, and we do not sell your personal information.

Information kept on your device

Your game progress, settings, coins, ad skips, energy and the end date of a No ads subscription are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Game Center

On iPhone and iPad the app can sign you in to Apple Game Center. Apple then gives the app your Game Center player ID and display name, which the app keeps on your device to show who is playing. We do not send them anywhere. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; you can sign out in the Settings app.

Purchases

The optional "No ads" subscription is sold and billed by Apple (App Store). We never see your payment details. The app only receives whether your subscription is active and when it ends. You can manage or cancel it in your App Store account settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app offers optional rewarded video ads (for example, a hint or extra energy in exchange for watching an ad). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

  • device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and an identifier for the app on your device (IDFV);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads, and crash and performance data about the ads.

On iPhone you are asked whether the app may track you; if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized based on it. Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for it first using Google's consent form. You can change the tracking choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

Because we do not store your information on our own servers, most of it can be controlled from your device: allow or deny tracking, sign out of Game Center, cancel the subscription, or delete the app. For requests about data collected by Google or Apple, you can also use their privacy tools linked above. For anything else, write to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
